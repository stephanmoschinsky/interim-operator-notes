# Decision Log — Market Entry

## Purpose of the Decision Log
This decision log records material decisions taken during a market entry mandate.

Its purpose is not documentation for its own sake,
but to make responsibility and decision patterns visible over time.

Only decisions with structural or irreversible impact are logged.
Activities and discussions are intentionally excluded.

## How Decisions Are Logged
Each decision entry captures:

- the decision that had to be made
- the context in which it was taken
- the constraints at the time
- the rationale
- the immediate consequence

Decisions are logged when taken,
not retrospectively optimised.

## Decision Entries
**Decision:** Pause all local hiring activities.

**Context:** Initial market signals remained indirect and inconsistent.
Role definitions were still evolving.

**Constraints:** Pressure to demonstrate momentum.
Expectations set with external stakeholders.

**Rationale:** Hiring would have converted uncertainty into fixed cost
and reinforced assumptions that had not yet been validated.

**Immediate Consequence:** Reduced activity visibility.
Preserved flexibility and decision space.


**Decision:** Centralise first customer conversations under interim ownership.

**Context:** Multiple parallel conversations created fragmented feedback.
Sales narratives diverged across functions.

**Constraints:** Existing relationships owned by headquarters teams.

**Rationale:** Direct signal quality was prioritised over distributed engagement.
Early learning required consistency rather than coverage.

**Immediate Consequence:** Slower outreach.
Higher clarity of market feedback.
