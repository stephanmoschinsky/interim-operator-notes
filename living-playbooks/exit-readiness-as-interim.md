# Exit Readiness — Interim Role

## Purpose of Exit Readiness
Exit readiness defines when an interim role can end responsibly.

It is not tied to timelines,
project milestones,
or subjective comfort.

Exit readiness exists
to ensure that responsibility can be carried
without interim presence.

## Exit Is a Structural Question
An interim exit is not a personal decision.

It is a structural question:
- Can decisions be taken without proxy?
- Can uncertainty be carried without escalation?
- Can correction happen without permission theatre?

If the structure holds,
exit becomes possible.

## Conditions That Indicate Exit Readiness
Several conditions indicate that an interim role can end:

- Decision ownership is explicit and exercised
- Escalation is used selectively, not routinely
- Market signals are acted upon directly
- Operating rhythm functions without interim enforcement

## Conditions That Mask Non-Readiness
Certain signals create the illusion of readiness:

- High activity with stable reporting
- Polite alignment without decision clarity
- Reliance on individual effort
- Informal assurances replacing mandate clarity

These conditions delay exit
while increasing risk.

## The Risk of Staying Too Long
Staying beyond exit readiness carries risk.

The interim role can become a substitute for ownership.
Decisions shift back into proxy patterns.
The organisation adapts to interim presence
instead of consolidating responsibility.

At this point, continued presence reduces value.

## The Risk of Leaving Too Early
Leaving too early also carries risk.

Responsibility may not yet be internalised.
Decision authority may exist on paper only.
Structural gaps may be masked by goodwill.

Exit readiness requires evidence,
not intention.

## How Exit Readiness Is Tested
Exit readiness is tested through absence.

- Decisions taken without interim input
- Corrections made without reassurance
- Issues surfaced without fear of consequence

If these behaviours persist,
exit is not disruptive.

## Final Question
Before exiting, one question matters:

Can this organisation carry the next phase
without substituting responsibility
through effort, escalation, or delay?

If yes, exit is timely.
If not, exit is premature.
