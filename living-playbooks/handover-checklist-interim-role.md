# Handover Checklist — Interim Role

## Purpose of the Handover
The purpose of a handover is not continuity of presence,
but continuity of responsibility.

A successful interim role ends
when ownership is transferred without loss of clarity.

## Preconditions for Handover
A handover should only begin when:

- Decision authority is clearly assigned
- Mandates and role boundaries are explicit
- Open issues are visible rather than implicit
- Remaining uncertainty is acknowledged, not hidden

Handover without these conditions
creates the illusion of stability.

## Ownership Transfer
Ownership transfer requires more than documentation.

The following must be explicitly transferred:
- Decision rights
- Escalation paths
- Stop authority
- Risk ownership

Anything that cannot be named
cannot be handed over.

## Decision Landscape
The incoming owner must have visibility into:

- Decisions already taken
- Decisions deliberately deferred
- Decisions that remain structurally unresolved

This prevents re-litigation
and false reopening of closed questions.

## Operating Rhythm
The operating rhythm must be clear and sustainable:

- Weekly review cadence
- Decision checkpoints
- Escalation thresholds
- Communication routines

The goal is not replication of the interim setup,
but operational viability.


## Market and External Interfaces
External interfaces must be stabilised:

- Clear ownership of customer relationships
- Explicit handover of partner commitments
- Visibility into external expectations

Unclear external ownership
creates immediate operational risk.

## Signals That the Role Can End
The interim role can end when:

- Decisions are taken without proxy escalation
- Responsibility is accepted rather than negotiated
- Market signals are acted upon directly
- The organisation no longer depends on interim presence

Staying beyond this point reduces value.

## Common Handover Traps
Common traps during handover include:

- Over-documentation instead of responsibility transfer
- Polite avoidance of unresolved issues
- Accelerated exit to maintain harmony
- Implicit expectations of continued availability

These traps undermine the purpose of interim work.

## Final Check
Before ending the interim role, one question must be answered:

Can the organisation carry this responsibility
without substituting it through effort or escalation?

If the answer is unclear,
the handover is premature.
