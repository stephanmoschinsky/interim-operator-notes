# Go / No-Go Memo — DACH Market Entry

## Context
The organisation had decided to enter the DACH market as its first European expansion.

Initial assumptions around market demand, product relevance, and timing were positive.
Internal alignment existed at board level, and a preliminary budget had been earmarked.

However, no operational market presence existed.
Customer feedback was indirect.
Decision-making remained largely centralised outside the region.

## Decision at Hand
The decision was whether to proceed with a full DACH market entry
or to pause further commitments until operational reality could be validated.

This decision had to be made before structural and financial commitments
would materially limit optionality.

## Irreversible Commitments
Proceeding would have required several irreversible commitments:

- Legal entity setup
- Senior local leadership hire
- Long-term budget allocation
- Formal go-to-market signalling towards customers and partners

Once initiated, these steps would have been difficult to reverse
without reputational and organisational cost.
